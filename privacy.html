Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how Niebla Relatos (“we”) handles data when you connect TikTok (and optionally YouTube/Instagram) to publish content.

1. Data we process

OAuth access and refresh tokens; basic TikTok profile identifiers needed to publish; video files and post metadata you choose to upload (title/caption, privacy, interaction settings, commercial disclosure).

2. Purpose

We use this data only to authenticate your account, show creator info before posting, and publish or schedule videos you approve.

3. Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept while the integration remains active. You can disconnect TikTok at any time; after revocation we stop using the tokens and delete them from our configuration when you request it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share only what is required with TikTok’s APIs (and other platforms you authorize) to complete publishing.
