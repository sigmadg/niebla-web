Privacy Policy

Niebla Relatos uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API only to upload videos on behalf of the account you approve (for example @misterios_niebla).

We store access and refresh tokens on a private local machine. We do not sell personal data. We do not collect data from other TikTok users.

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
